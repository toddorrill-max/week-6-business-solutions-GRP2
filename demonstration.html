Request: “Update my address”

Maria Lopez (fictional customer) has moved. Trace the proposed rule through the workflow. This illustration does not submit a banking request.

  1. 1

    Business Solution Skill

    Wrote the rule: reuse her name, phone and email; ask only for the new address; within 1 business day (est.); guarantee covered.

  2. 2

    Customer experience

    The rule identifies 3 details to reuse and asks only for the new address. This trace illustrates the proposed customer experience.

  4. 4

Business solution

tell-us-once-rules (custom Skill)

Decides, for each task a customer starts, what the bank already knows, what it still needs, how long it takes and whether the $10 guarantee applies.

The Skill's self-check passed all six items. It also flagged four concerns on its own, including that its wording overstated what the law requires for two tasks. Documented prompt, output summary, and verification notes: evidence/business-skill.

The Skill's rules became rules.json v1.1. This file documents the proposed customer-checker rules, separate from the Page 2 report planner. The team checked the output independently and found a defect the Skill’s self-check missed.
