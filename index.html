01 / The Problem and Why It Matters

One Reliable Answer.
Faster Service.
Fewer Repeats.

Getting help from your bank shouldn’t mean providing the same information again and again.

Our teams currently work across more than 1,000 interconnected workbooks each quarter to locate, compare, and reconcile information. That complicated process can slow answers, create duplicate requests, and increase the chance of errors.

We’re building a better way.

Our proposed solution uses AI-assisted mapping and automated reporting to connect information, reduce duplication, and give employees one reliable source for serving customers.

The problem

Too Many Files. Too Many Repeats.

Customer information may move through multiple teams and many connected files. Each handoff can mean another search, another check, or another request for details the customer already provided.

  1. 01

    Information provided

    The customer shares the details needed to get help.

  2. 02

    Information requested again

    Another team may ask for the same information.

  3. 03

    Files searched and reconciled

    Employees compare information across multiple files.

  4. 04

    The customer waits

    An answer depends on completing those checks.

1,000+

Workbooks linked, checked, and reconciled every quarter.

Up to 3 days

Complex answers may take up to 3 business days.

As many as 4 times

The same customer information may be requested again.

Why it matters

Every repeat costs someone time.

Customers feel the delay. Employees carry the extra work. Both deserve a simpler way to get to a reliable answer.

For our customers

  • Repeating information already provided.
  • Waiting longer for an answer.
  • Potentially receiving inconsistent information from different teams.
  • Spending more time dealing with the process instead of getting help.

For our employees

Employees spend time searching, matching, checking, and reconciling information instead of helping customers. Multiple files make it harder to know which information is current and which answer to trust.

Time spent connecting files is time that could be spent connecting with people.

Our proposed solution

From 1,000+ Workbooks
to One Reliable Answer

Connect the information behind the scenes, so teams can focus on the customer in front of them.

  1. 01 / MAP

    Understand the connections.

    AI-assisted mapping identifies workbook connections, dependencies, and duplicate information.

  2. 02 / AUTOMATE

    Make reporting routine.

    Automated scheduled reports provide teams with the information they need, reducing repeated manual preparation.

  3. 03 / UNIFY

    Work from the same source.

    One reliable source replaces unnecessary duplicate files and gives teams consistent, current information.
